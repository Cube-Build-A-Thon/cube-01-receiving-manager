When palletized goods arrive from overseas manufacturers at your warehouse receiving dock, operators perform spot checks at best. By the time damage, unit count shortages, or wrong-variant defects are discovered weeks later in FBA prep or during customer returns, your supplier refuses reimbursement because no authoritative proof was recorded on arrival.

Today, we are introducing **Receiving Manager**—a vision-based AI agent that transforms your receiving dock into an undeniable chain of custody:

1. **Single-Pass Point-of-Receipt Verification**: Evaluates carton crushing, water damage, tears, unit count shortages, and PO specification compliance in sub-second latency (6.16ms local heuristic execution; <1,000ms SLA) from photos taken right as cartons are opened.
2. **First-Class Uncertainty & Fail-Open**: If photographs are blurry or glare-covered, the agent explicitly flags `UNCERTAIN` rather than guessing, preserving warehouse flow without blocking dock lines.
3. **Cross-Pod Interoperability**: Generates standardized Evidence Contract v1.1 JSON records directly consumed by Prep Manager (Step 02) and Recovery Manager (Step 05) for automated supplier chargebacks.

With Receiving Manager, every pallet that enters your building leaves behind an immutable visual birth certificate.
